Slyce Stack: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Slyce Stack: Sky Tower ("the app") is a game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or photos.

Information stored on your device

Your best score is saved only on your device so it can be shown the next time you play. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Third-party services

The app does not currently use analytics, advertising, or other third-party services that collect data. If this changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
