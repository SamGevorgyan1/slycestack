Slyce Stack: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Slyce Stack: Sky Tower ("the app") is a game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location, and the app does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Your best score and whether you bought "Remove Ads" are saved only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown and tapped) and basic device and app information
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information related to ads

If you do not allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your IDFA. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown and can change their choice using the "Privacy" button on the main menu.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
